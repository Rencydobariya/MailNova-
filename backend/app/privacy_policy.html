Privacy Policy

Effective October 17, 2026

MailNova helps you organize your Gmail inbox. This policy explains what information is used to provide MailNova, when it is shared with service providers, and how you can clear your data or disconnect your Google account.

Information used by MailNova

When you connect Gmail, MailNova handles the information needed to show and organize your messages:

  • Google account details: your verified email address and an account identifier used to keep your MailNova session and local cache associated with the right account.
  • Email details: sender, subject, date, snippet, message content, message and thread identifiers, and Gmail labels such as unread, starred, important, or spam.
  • AI feature inputs and results: relevant email text and details, a search query you enter, or context for a reply suggestion; plus the resulting summary, category, priority or spam assessment.
  • Preferences and service usage: settings saved by the extension and limited AI request counts used to enforce service limits. The backend stores a hashed account identifier with those counts; it does not put email text in the usage database.

How information is used and shared

MailNova uses Gmail information to retrieve and display messages, support search and organization, generate summaries and analysis, and provide user-requested email actions. MailNova does not sell Gmail data or use it for advertising.

The extension sends authenticated requests to the MailNova backend, hosted by Render. Gmail requests are made through Google APIs. When an AI feature runs—including automatic analysis when enabled—the backend may send relevant message text, sender or subject details, a search query, or reply context to the Google Gemini API. Gemini returns a result to MailNova for display. MailNova does not send a reply until you choose to send it. Choosing to mark a message read or unread updates that message in Gmail.

Google Gemini processing: Google's handling of prompts and responses depends on the service tier used by MailNova's API project. Google's current terms state that content submitted to unpaid Gemini services may be used to improve Google products and may be reviewed by people; paid services have different terms and may retain prompts and responses for limited safety monitoring. MailNova does not control Google's processing or retention. See the Gemini API terms for details.

Storage and retention

The extension saves email and AI-result caches in Chrome local storage so your workspace can load without repeating the same requests. These items remain until replaced, removed with Chrome extension data, or cleared when you choose Disconnect and clear local email data in MailNova settings. Disconnecting also clears MailNova's cached Google sign-in tokens. Your Gmail messages remain in Gmail unless you change or delete them there.

MailNova's backend processes message content to complete a request and does not intentionally save full email contents in its usage database. That database contains hashed account identifiers and request counts for rate limiting; older counters are removed during later quota checks. Render may process limited operational information under its own service terms.

Google's retention of Gemini prompts and responses is governed by the terms for the API service tier in use. Review Google's terms before using AI features with sensitive or confidential messages.

Your choices

  • You can disconnect Google and clear MailNova's local email and AI caches in the extension's Google Account settings.
  • You can revoke MailNova's Google access from your Google Account security settings.
  • You can choose whether to use AI-assisted features. When an AI feature runs, the relevant inputs are sent to Google Gemini as described above.

Security

Requests between the extension and MailNova's backend use HTTPS. Google access tokens are verified and used for the current request; MailNova does not intentionally store them in its backend database. Internet services cannot eliminate every security risk, but MailNova is designed to limit backend storage of credentials and email content.

Google API data

MailNova's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
