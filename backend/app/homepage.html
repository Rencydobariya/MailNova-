Privacy in plain language

Know what happens to your email data.

MailNova sends the message details needed for a requested feature to its backend. When an AI feature runs, relevant content may be sent to Google Gemini to produce the result. MailNova does not sell Gmail data or use it for advertising.

  • Email replies are sent only after you choose Send.
  • MailNova's local cache can be cleared in the extension settings.
  • You can revoke Google account access from your Google Account settings.
